
# Pankaj Kanti Jodder

### Geospatial Data Scientist | GIScience & GeoAI Researcher | PhD Candidate

**University of North Carolina at Charlotte | Charlotte, NC, USA**

I am a PhD candidate in Geography at the University of North Carolina at Charlotte, specializing in **GIScience, geospatial data science, remote sensing, spatial statistics, and applied machine learning**.

My research integrates geospatial programming, Earth observation, spatial modeling, and computational methods to understand complex urban and environmental systems. I develop reproducible geospatial workflows, process large spatial datasets, and translate analytical findings into maps, interactive visualizations, and decision-support applications.

My background combines academic research with applied GIS and data analytics experience across urban planning, environmental assessment, transportation, healthcare accessibility, and geospatial data management.

## Research & Scholarly Contributions

My research spans GIScience, urban and environmental analytics, remote sensing, and spatial modeling, with contributions to peer-reviewed journal articles, book chapters, and collaborative interdisciplinary research.

My published work addresses urban livability, land-use transformation, urban heat islands, carbon footprints, vegetation dynamics, climate-related hazards, and environmental sustainability.

My doctoral research investigates how **three-dimensional urban morphology can extend conventional two-dimensional approaches to understanding metropolitan structure**, using geospatial analysis, spatial statistics, and machine learning.

I have also contributed to research involving spatial accessibility, urban infrastructure, environmental vulnerability, and geographically varying relationships between socioeconomic and environmental conditions.
